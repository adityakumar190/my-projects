Research Interests

Projects

Text analysis

Synthetic Reading of O. Henry’s “The Gift of the Magi”

A reader-oriented study of how the narrator’s language turns economic loss into emotional value. A close reading of sampled passages was combined with language-model extraction of 152 verified phrases across four dimensions, counted across 13 segments and four stages, and examined with chi-square and Spearman tests. Vocabulary of money and counting is concentrated in the opening, and vocabulary of wisdom in the final paragraph.

Retrieval-augmented generation

Extraction of Indian Cultural Elements Using RAG

Retrieval-augmented generation was used to extract the flora and fauna named in six Indian literary texts from Project Gutenberg, producing 117 entries (70 flora, 47 fauna). Elements recurring across the books include the mango, palm, lotus, nightingale and crow. The lists are documented as a first draft for expert review, with measurement of precision and recall as the next step.

NLP resource

Hindi and Bhojpuri Stop Words for NLP

Two non-overlapping stop-word lists of 100 words each, written in Roman letters and shared as Python files for text preprocessing. A comparison by grammatical category shows how the two languages use postpositions, pronouns and auxiliary verbs.

AI tool design

CiteCheck: APA 7 and MLA 9 Citation Auditor

A skill, written as a single Markdown file, that guides a language-model assistant in auditing in-text citations and reference lists. It has eight modules and fourteen command switches, and each finding rests on the supplied document and the sources consulted. Testing on papers with known citation issues is the planned next step.

Sentiment analysis

Sentiment Analysis of YouTube Comments

Sentiment analysis of YouTube comments on two space mission narratives: the story of Laika, the first dog sent into space, and the Soviet cosmonaut Vladimir Komarov, who died during a space mission. A rule-based method (TextBlob) and a transformer-based model (Twitter-RoBERTa) are compared to understand audience responses to human and animal space mission tragedies.
